The method

BRIEF: five parts of a prompt that works

BBackgroundWho you are, the business, what the file is
RRequestThe job, and the problems you care about
IInstructionsRules: accounts to use, what must not change
EExpected outputTable, file or memo, with the columns you need
FFact-checkTotals to tie out; flag anything uncertain

Questions

Before you enrol

Do I need to know coding or advanced Excel?

No. You need basic accounting knowledge and a computer with a browser. You'll type instructions in plain English.

Which version of Claude do I need?

You can follow the course with a Claude account that lets you upload files. Some features, such as creating spreadsheet files, depend on your plan. Each lesson shows a way to do the task either way.

Is it safe to use client data?

The course uses a fictional company, so the practice files are safe to upload. Lesson 1.5 covers how to follow your firm's AI policy and remove personal data before using real files.

Is the certificate an official Claude or Anthropic certification?

No. It's a certificate of completion from BrightLedger Academy, an independent training provider. This course is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

How long do I have access?

Lifetime access, including future updates to the lessons and practice files.

Will the examples match Malaysian practice?

Examples use ringgit and a Malaysian café, with SST and EPF, SOCSO and EIS accounts in the chart of accounts. The methods work under any accounting framework.
